## What it does

Stress-tests an idea through focused questions, recommendations, and evidence. Ordinary grilling stops when the remaining uncertainty no longer blocks the outcome; exhaustive exploration is available on request.

## When to reach for it

Ask to be grilled or to stress-test a plan, or invoke `$grilling` in Codex or `/grilling` in Claude Code.

## Common questions

**Will it make me decide every detail?**

No. It asks about consequential goals and tradeoffs, looks up facts itself, and states routine assumptions.

**Can I still ask for relentless questioning?**

Yes. Explicitly request exhaustive grilling and it explores the relevant decision tree at that depth.

## It's working if

- Questions have recommendations and respect earlier answers.
- Dependent questions wait until their prerequisites are settled.
- Authorized follow-on work continues without a duplicate confirmation.

## Where it fits

grill-me provides a discussion-only shortcut. grill-with-docs also records settled project terminology and decisions. See the [collection guide](https://github.com/zac-dougo/zd-skills/blob/main/README.md) for related workflows.
